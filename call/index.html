Information for Authors

Submission site
Submissions and reviews will be managed through Microsoft CMT. Authors must have a Microsoft CMT account before submitting a paper. The WoMAPF 2027 submission link will be announced here.
Format and length
Submissions should follow the AAAI-27 format. There is no page limit.
Eligible work
New, ongoing, recently published, or concurrently reviewed work. Prior or concurrent submissions may use their original format or a shorter version but must be clearly labeled and comply with applicable copyright and venue policies.
Review process
All submissions will undergo light review for quality and relevance to the workshop. The review process is single-blind with at least two reviews per submission; authors would not need to anonymize their papers.
Publication
The workshop is non-archival. Workshop notes or an online collection will document the work presented. Final-material submission instructions and the camera-ready deadline will be announced.
Presentation
The workshop will be held in person in Montréal, Canada. Paper presentations are planned for 10 minutes, including 1–2 minutes for questions. A poster session will take place during lunch; software and hardware demonstrations are subject to venue facilities. Presentation assignments and preparation instructions will be announced. Any remote participation option is subject to AAAI approval.
