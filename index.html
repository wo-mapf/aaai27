WoMAPF Scope

Multi-Agent Path Finding (MAPF) studies how multiple agents can reach assigned goals through shared spaces without collisions. It underpins warehouse automation, logistics, autonomous transport, service robotics, manufacturing, and large-scale fleet coordination, yet remains computationally demanding: common objectives such as sum of costs and makespan are NP-hard, while real systems impose tight planning and execution constraints. WoMAPF brings together researchers across AI planning, search, robotics, machine learning, theoretical computer science, operations research, and multi-agent systems to advance theory, algorithms, benchmarks, and deployed systems—from classical, decentralized, and learning-guided methods to lifelong MAPF, task allocation, replanning under uncertainty, and robot-specific constraints.
